My Story Writing Adventure

A Creative Writing Journal for Kids Ages 7–12

What if writing a story could feel less like an assignment and more like an adventure?

My Story Writing Adventure gives young writers a clear, encouraging path through the story-writing process. Children can develop an idea, plan what happens, write their story, and reflect on what they created.

What children experience

A structured path without taking away the imagination.

The journal is built around repeated story-writing adventures. Each adventure gives children space to think about their idea before they write, develop the important parts of the story, and look back at what they have created.

Plan the idea

Give the story a direction before the writing begins.

Write the story

Turn the plan into original writing in a dedicated space.

Reflect & grow

Look back, notice progress, and build writing confidence.
